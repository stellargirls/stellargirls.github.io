Office, practice plates, and board specials

Hub + match-3 · Windows PC

Enter Agency lands you at the desk. The comeback board sits on the blotter; season, concept, and the next chart week are in the room with you. The rest of the floor — Practice, Break Room, Memory Archive — is the entryway on the left. You don’t visit the office as a shortcut anymore. You live in it.

Practice opens on an empty studio. Pick a girl and she’s on that same floor: Ha-eun, Soo-yeon, Dae-ri, Ji-na, Min-ji, each with her own training plate, marks in the mirror matching the body in the room.

On stage, special shapes leave power-ups on the gem. Four in a line is a Line or Column Blast (same chevron, rotated). A 2×2 or T is Burst. Five in a line is Nova. The marks sit in the center of the jewel — swap them or tap them to fire. Shine and specialty still flash; Stage Clear still gets its beat before the result card.
